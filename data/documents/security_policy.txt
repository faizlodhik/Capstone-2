NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Review date: December 31, 2025
Policy owner: Maya Chen, Director of Security Operations

PURPOSE
This policy defines the minimum information-security requirements for Northstar Analytics,
a fictional company. It applies to Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and Operations.

ACCESS MANAGEMENT
All workforce members must use company-managed accounts and multifactor authentication.
Access reviews must be completed quarterly. The target is 100% completion within 10 business
days of each quarter-end. Departing personnel must have access removed within four hours
of confirmed departure notification.

DATA CLASSIFICATION
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer
contracts, credentials, employee records, and production data are Restricted. Restricted
data may be accessed only by authorized personnel with a documented business need.

SECURITY INCIDENTS
Suspected security incidents must be reported to Security Operations immediately. Critical
incidents require acknowledgement within 30 minutes, containment within four hours when
practicable, and a documented post-incident review within 10 business days.

ENGINEERING CONTROLS
Production changes require approved code review, automated testing, and deployment logging.
Secrets must not be stored in source code. Vulnerability findings rated Critical or High
must have an owner and remediation plan within two business days.

TRAINING AND EVIDENCE
Security training is required annually, with a target completion rate of at least 98%.
Evidence includes access-review records, training reports, incident records, and deployment
logs. Maya Chen is responsible for policy monitoring and quarterly reporting.
