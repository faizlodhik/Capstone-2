NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK REFERENCE
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Owner: Elena Brooks, Director of People Operations

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
All values in this document are synthetic comparison benchmarks created solely for testing
retrieval, quantitative analysis, and recommendation workflows. They are not authoritative
external research, market statistics, or Deloitte guidance.

COMPARISON BENCHMARKS
Employee satisfaction benchmark: 75 on a 0–100 scale.
Monthly customer churn benchmark: 2.5% of customers_start.
Average complaint-resolution benchmark: 5.0 business days.
Support-case resolution benchmark: 90% of cases resolved within the reporting period.

COMPARISON METHOD
Compare Northstar Analytics results with these synthetic values only after confirming the
metric definition, period, population, and denominator. Employee satisfaction should use
employee_satisfaction_score. Churn should use churned_customers divided by
customers_start. Support resolution should use resolved_cases divided by support_cases.
Average resolution should use average_resolution_days.

INTERPRETATION
A result above or below a benchmark is an analytical signal, not proof of a cause. Analysts
must consider region, product, customer segment, reporting period, and data completeness.
Recommendations should be connected to documented company policies and customer-success
strategies.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
The benchmarks are not validated against external sources. The dataset is fictional, and
the employee satisfaction score is repeated across operational rows for testing convenience.
