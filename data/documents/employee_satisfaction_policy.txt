NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION POLICY
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Policy owner: Elena Brooks, Director of People Operations

MEASUREMENT
Employee satisfaction is measured on a 0–100 scale through a quarterly anonymous survey.
The score is the average of valid responses across Engineering, Sales, Customer Success,
and Operations. The company target is at least 78.

GOVERNANCE
People Operations administers the survey during the final month of each quarter. Results
are segmented by department and reported at company level. Results with fewer than five
responses are suppressed to protect confidentiality.

ACTION THRESHOLDS
A score below 70 requires a documented improvement plan within 30 days. A decline of five
or more points from the prior quarter requires leadership review. Improvement plans should
address workload, manager effectiveness, career development, tools, or operating processes.

RELEVANT POLICIES
Employee satisfaction may be affected by code-review workload, customer escalation volume,
sales target pressure, training requirements, and access to appropriate work tools. These
relationships are hypotheses for analysis and are not proof of causation.

DATA SOURCE
The business_metrics.csv file contains an employee_satisfaction_score field using the
0–100 scale. The value is synthetic and repeated across operational rows for analytical
testing. Elena Brooks owns survey governance and quarterly action tracking.
