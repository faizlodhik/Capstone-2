NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — CUSTOMER SUCCESS STRATEGY
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Strategy owner: Priya Nair, Director of Customer Success

OBJECTIVE
Customer Success supports retention and adoption for Core Platform and Analytics Pro across
North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The strategy applies to Enterprise, Mid-Market,
and SMB customer segments.

CUSTOMER HEALTH
Customer health is assessed using product adoption, support activity, open escalations,
renewal timing, and executive engagement. A declining health signal requires an owner and
next action within five business days.

RETENTION TARGETS
The operating target is to reduce avoidable customer churn while maintaining responsive
service. Monthly churn rate is calculated as churned_customers divided by customers_start.
The business_metrics.csv file contains both fields and customers_end for reconciliation.

SERVICE TARGETS
Customer Success targets acknowledgement of complaints within one business day and standard
resolution within five business days. Support trends should be reviewed with revenue,
customer counts, and segment information rather than in isolation.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
For repeated complaints, Customer Success should coordinate a root-cause review with
Engineering. For declining adoption, the account team should provide enablement or a
success plan. For high-risk renewals, Sales and Customer Success should agree on an owner,
next milestone, and customer communication plan.

REPORTING
Priya Nair reviews monthly retention, support, and resolution metrics. The strategy should
be adjusted when a policy creates avoidable customer friction or when service performance
falls below the documented thresholds.
