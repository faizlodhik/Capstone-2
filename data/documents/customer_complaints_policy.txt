NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS POLICY
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Policy owner: Priya Nair, Director of Customer Success

PURPOSE
This policy defines how Northstar Analytics receives, records, investigates, resolves, and
escalates customer complaints for Core Platform and Analytics Pro.

DEFINITIONS
A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction that requires investigation or corrective
action. A complaint is distinct from a standard support request, although a support request
may become a complaint if service expectations are not met.

REQUIRED RESPONSE TIMES
Customer Success must acknowledge a complaint within one business day. The target is to
resolve standard complaints within five business days. Complaints involving security,
privacy, billing, or potential contractual impact must be escalated to the appropriate
functional owner on the same business day.

WORKFLOW
1. Record the customer, product, region, issue category, priority, and received date.
2. Assign an owner and acknowledge the complaint.
3. Investigate the cause using support history and customer context.
4. Provide a documented response and corrective action.
5. Obtain customer confirmation when appropriate and close the record.
6. Review trends monthly for systemic improvements.

QUALITY THRESHOLDS
At least 95% of complaints should receive acknowledgement within one business day.
At least 90% of standard complaints should be resolved within five business days.
Complaints reopened by the customer require a manager review within two business days.

MEASUREMENT
The business_metrics.csv file contains support_cases, resolved_cases, and
average_resolution_days. These fields support trend analysis but do not replace individual
complaint records. Priya Nair owns monthly reporting and corrective-action tracking.
