NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — SALES PERFORMANCE POLICY
Document status: Synthetic test data
Effective date: January 1, 2024
Policy owner: Marcus Lee, Vice President of Sales

SCOPE
This policy governs sales performance reporting for Core Platform and Analytics Pro across
North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. It covers Enterprise, Mid-Market, and SMB segments.

METRICS
Revenue is recorded as a fictional monthly amount in the business_metrics.csv file.
Customers_start, new_customers, churned_customers, and customers_end support customer-base
analysis. The reconciliation formula is customers_end = customers_start + new_customers
- churned_customers.

PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Sales performance is reviewed monthly and summarized quarterly. Regional comparisons should
consider product mix, customer segment, new-customer volume, churn, and customer-success
outcomes. Q4 means October, November, and December.

OPERATING EXPECTATIONS
Sales teams must maintain accurate opportunity and customer records. Material forecast
changes require an explanation. Customer commitments involving product capability, service
levels, or implementation timing must be coordinated with Customer Success and Engineering.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS LINKAGE
Sales performance is not evaluated only on new revenue. Leadership should also review
churned customers, support workload, resolution time, and employee satisfaction. A policy
change should be considered when sales incentives appear to encourage commitments that
increase complaints, support cases, or avoidable churn.

GOVERNANCE
Marcus Lee owns monthly performance reporting. Priya Nair provides customer-success context,
and Elena Brooks provides relevant employee-satisfaction trends.
